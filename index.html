About the Municipality of Baungon

Baungon is a municipality in the province of Bukidnon, Mindanao, nestled among mountains, farmland, and river valleys. Founded in 1956, the town has grown from an agricultural frontier settlement into a community shaped by farming, trade, and a strong sense of local identity, reflected in the mountains, fields, flora, and carabao depicted on the municipal seal.

Mission — To deliver responsive, transparent, and inclusive governance that uplifts the welfare of every resident of Baungon.

Vision — A progressive, peaceful, and self-reliant municipality anchored on sustainable agriculture, good governance, and community well-being.
